TEXAS TEMPORARY TAG FRAUD PENALTIES
Source: Texas Transportation Code Chapter 503 and Texas Occupations Code Chapter 2301

1\. CRIMINAL PENALTIES FOR TAG FRAUD

1.1 Fraudulent Temporary Tag Issuance
Issuing temporary tags fraudulently is a criminal offense under Texas law.
\- Class A misdemeanor for first offense: up to $4,000 fine and/or up to 1 year in county jail
\- State jail felony for subsequent offenses: 180 days to 2 years in state jail, up to $10,000 fine
\- Third degree felony if fraud involves 25+ tags: 2 to 10 years in prison

1.2 Administrative Penalties
\- Administrative penalties up to $10,000 per violation per day
\- Immediate suspension of dealer license and webDEALER access
\- Revocation of General Distinguishing Number (GDN)

2\. COMMON VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

2.1 Tag-Related Violations
\- Issuing tags for vehicles not in dealer inventory: Class A misdemeanor
\- Issuing tags without a valid sale transaction: Class A misdemeanor
\- Exceeding annual tag limit without authorization: administrative penalty up to $10,000
\- Failing to submit title paperwork within 30 days: administrative penalty

2.2 Dealer Plate Violations
\- Using dealer plates for personal transportation: administrative penalty
\- Allowing non-employees to use dealer plates: administrative penalty
\- Failure to return plates upon license cancellation: Class C misdemeanor

3\. ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

3.1 License Actions
The TxDMV may take the following actions against violating dealers:
\- Warning letter for minor first-time violations
\- Agreed order with corrective action requirements
\- Suspension of dealer license (temporary)
\- Revocation of dealer license (permanent)
\- Denial of license renewal

3.2 Reporting Violations
Violations can be reported to:
\- TxDMV Enforcement Division: (888) 368-4689
\- TxDMV online complaint form at www.txdmv.gov
\- Local law enforcement for criminal violations
